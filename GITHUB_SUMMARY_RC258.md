## WAFD ONE 10.0.0 RC258 — Bilingual Quotations and Direct Driver Launch

RC258 restores the mobile quotation-preview back button with safe-area and top-layer handling, adds persisted Arabic/English quotation output with editable English commercial text, and keeps preview, print and share in the chosen language. It also changes the installed-app launch target to the canonical role home and redirects legacy WAFD launch URLs so driver accounts bypass Page Not Found and intermediate application screens.
