Simplify Storekeeper into three practical searchable workflows

- Replaced the duplicated technical Stock Movement entry cards with three guided Storekeeper tasks: purchase receipt/distribution, employee handover, and inventory information.
- Added category-first and server-backed material search so long unfiltered item lists are never shown.
- Added a multi-item receipt flow with editable quantity, unit price, destination warehouse/fridge, and optional expiry date.
- Added role-first employee handover for Cleaning Supervisor, Production/Cooking Supervisor or Chef, Project Manager, Quality Inspector, and Delivery Supervisor.
- Displayed recipients by full name while retaining the user ID only as an internal value.
- Limited handover selection to stock that is actually available in the chosen source location.
- Preserved Cleaning Supervisor pending-receipt acknowledgement for cleaning warehouse issues.
- Added inventory summary counters, warehouse/search filters, low/zero-stock views, and 30-day expiry alerts.
- Preserved audit Stock Movements and blocked negative prices, duplicate items, invalid recipients, and quantities above available stock.
