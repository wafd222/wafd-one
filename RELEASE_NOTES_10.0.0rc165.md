# WAFD ONE 10.0.0rc165

## Main Dashboard Restoration + In-Dashboard Section Navigation

- Restores the approved WAFD ONE Operations Command Center as the main company interface.
- Keeps the original black/gold hero, action cards, operational flow, management KPIs, projects, alerts, deliveries and executive sections.
- Adds six clickable section cards inside the same dashboard: Operations, Inventory & Purchasing, Delivery, Finance, Master Data, Documents & Undertakings.
- Moves low-level inventory/reference detail (materials, warehouses/cold rooms, consumption lists and hot-cabinet detail) out of the main page and keeps it accessible through the section hubs.
- Changes the Frappe v16 WAFD ONE sidebar Home entry to open `wafd-one-dashboard` directly.
- Preserves RC164 Cleaning Supervisor workflow, Go-Live inventory preparation and all tested role permissions.
- No operational transaction, project, invoice, payment or stock balance is modified by migration.
