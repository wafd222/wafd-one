# WAFD ONE 10.0.0rc17

## Optional Hot Cabinets
- Added `WAFD Hot Cabinet` master with capacity, status, hotel, cleaning and maintenance data.
- Added optional Hot Cabinet section to Packaging Record.
- Hot cabinets are never mandatory and do not block packaging, loading or delivery when unused.
- Supports mixed packing: only part of the packed quantity may be assigned to cabinets.
- Validates duplicate cabinets, capacity, availability and total sandwiches.

## Hotel Undertaking
- Undertaking remains independent from Hot Cabinets and all operational workflows.
- Added `Hotel Requires Undertaking` option on each hotel.
- Added buttons on hotel records to create and view undertakings.
- New undertakings are linked only to the hotel; company/client, dates, meals, count and nationality are entered explicitly.
- Preserved the approved one-page undertaking text and design.
