# WAFD ONE 10.0.0 RC195

- Dedicated undertaking-only officer role.
- Officers can create, edit, approve, issue, save and share their own undertakings.
- Management-only preparer/approver audit fields with automatic names and timestamps.
- Reviewer role can review all undertakings and see who prepared each one.
- Undertaking-only role home for delegated staff.
- Existing undertakings are backfilled with their original creator from Frappe owner metadata.
