# WAFD ONE 10.0.0 RC196 — Undertaking Officer Template Lockdown

- Undertaking Officers can continue entering operational/beneficiary/hotel/service data and can preview, approve, issue, save and share the undertaking PDF.
- Undertaking Officers cannot edit or upload the company logo, signature, stamp, fixed terms, authorized signatory, signatory title, or signature/stamp visibility controls.
- Protected fields are enforced at DocField permission level and again server-side to block crafted API requests.
- Protected template sections are hidden from the Undertaking Officer form.
- System Manager and WAFD Operations Manager retain management access to protected undertaking identity/template fields.
- No change to the approved undertaking PDF design or RC194 terms.
