# WAFD ONE 10.0.0 RC205

## Mobile undertaking entry and preview fit

- Undertaking Officers are routed to the WAFD role home on the first mobile Desk load of a browser session instead of restoring an internal undertaking form as the entry screen.
- The undertaking HTML preview now automatically fits the complete document width to the mobile viewport.
- Added in-preview two-finger pinch zoom from 25% to 400% while keeping vertical/horizontal scrolling available when zoomed.
- Preview fit is recalculated after issuance and on orientation/viewport changes.
- No undertaking PDF, signature, stamp, terms, approval, save, or share logic was changed.
- No database schema change; migrate is not required.
