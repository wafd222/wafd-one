# WAFD ONE 10.0.0 RC210

## Undertaking officer navigation flow
- Prevent the admin-only Undertaking Team page from rendering for undertaking officers when a reused mobile browser restores the manager's previous route.
- Route undertaking officers immediately to their dedicated WAFD ONE home.
- Open a new Hotel Undertaking directly in the full Form instead of Quick Entry.
- Keep the newly created undertaking open after Save so the officer can continue directly to Preview, Issue, Save or Share.
- Preserve RC209 multi-user permissions, template lockdown, PDF workflow, contract number, signature, stamp and approved terms.
