# WAFD ONE 10.0.0 RC272

## Unified Android and iPhone employee home

- Shows the same WAFD ONE employee home on Android and iPhone.
- Activates the compact WAFD app bar in Android Chrome as well as installed PWAs.
- Restores the single global language selector on Android Home.
- Hides Frappe navigation, workspace sidebar, notification area, and unrelated workspace tasks on the employee home.
- Keeps only the cards assigned by the employee's WAFD role(s).
- Preserves the approved language globally for all employee screens.
- Supports narrow mobile viewports, Android desktop-mode viewports, and touch devices without changing the desktop management dashboard.

## Upgrade

Run **Migrate Site**, **Clear Cache**, and **Build Assets**, then sign out and sign in again. On Android, close all old WAFD tabs before reopening the site.
