# WAFD ONE 10.0.0 RC275

## Employee beneficiary access

- Adds **Delivery Data Viewer / متابعة بيانات التسليم** to Employee Management.
- The manager can create a beneficiary account with name, email, password and this task.
- The same account can receive additional WAFD tasks later through the existing multi-task editor.
- Delivery Supervisor assigns a delivery to one or more beneficiary accounts by name.
- The beneficiary signs in normally and sees only deliveries assigned to that account.
- The beneficiary page is strictly read-only and includes driver, mobile, plate, loading, departure, arrival, receiver and delivery photo.
- Removes public-link creation and expiry-date entry from the Delivery Supervisor workflow.

## Mobile date correction

- Forces native delivery date controls to use left-to-right numeric rendering inside Arabic dialogs.
- Prevents the day, month and year from visually merging or reversing on iPhone and Android.

## Upgrade

Run migrate, clear cache and restart after installing RC275.
