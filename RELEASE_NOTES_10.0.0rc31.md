# WAFD ONE 10.0.0rc31

- Opens or prepares the quality inspection immediately after production completion.
- Opens a CCP cooking-temperature check after a passed quality inspection.
- Prefills the production batch, inspector, time, unit, and configurable HACCP limits.
- Leaves only the measured value for the inspector to enter during the normal compliant path.
- Automatically verifies a compliant CCP check for authorized roles and releases the batch.
- Routes the user back to the released production batch, ready to continue to packaging.
- Noncompliant measurements remain blocked and still require deviation details, corrective action, and evidence according to settings.
- Adds an explicit **Awaiting Quality** production status so completing cooking no longer fails before quality and food-safety approval.
- The batch becomes **Ready** only after the compliant CCP measurement is verified and food-safety release succeeds.
