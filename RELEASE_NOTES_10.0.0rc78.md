# WAFD ONE 10.0.0 RC78

- Added WAFD Nationality master with 249 ISO 3166 countries/territories, Arabic and English names, and ISO codes.
- Changed undertaking beneficiary nationality to a selectable Link field.
- Added party nationality and clarified CR/passport field.
- Restricted Undertaking Quick Entry to the frequently changing fields: hotel, party/beneficiary name, CR/passport, party nationality, beneficiary count, beneficiary nationality, start date and end date.
- Changed mission country to the nationality master.
- Expanded ingredient categories for seafood, legumes, bakery, spices, oils, desserts, cleaning, hygiene and operating supplies.
- Added an operational seed library of food materials, packaging, detergents, hygiene supplies and operating tools.
- Added a multilingual/national Hajj catering recipe starter library, including Iftar meal records for the Prophet’s Mosque, Quba, Qiblatain and Dhul Hulayfah Miqat.
- Ensured the Indonesian Hajj mission record is available.

Important: seeded recipes and materials are operational templates. Supplier costs, exact recipe quantities, hotel licence data, and seasonal mission contact details must be verified before production use.
